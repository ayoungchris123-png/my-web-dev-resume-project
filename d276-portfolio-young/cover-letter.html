Dear FBI Agency

My name is Science. Johnny. Science. I need a job, and you need me. Let me tell you why.

2000 years ago, the Romans. In their society, there was a group of individuals with special powers, referred to as Augurs. Their job? To tell the future by reading the entrails of cows. Did it work? No clue. But I can tell you this: if you need to look into the future, you need Science. You. Need. Me. Here's what you're going to do. You're going to hire me at your top salary. You're going to give me this job. And I am going to make your lives ten THOUSAND times easier. After all, what can't you do when you have a guy who can see into the future with science? Oh, you don't know? Well, let me tell you. Nothing. NOTHING.

So, anyways. My job experience is pretty awesome. I've worked as a science teacher for a year as a favor to a friend. You know how it goes. Oh, the McDonalds thing? Yeah, that was totally just a cover for my super top-secret mission that I totally can't tell you about. Trust me when I say that it was for a totally awesome, super epic reason. To sum it up, there's a lot that I can do, and barely anything that I can't do. Just ask the manager at Micky D's, he can vouch for that. So I think that about covers it.

Guess I'll wrap it up with this: the next time you're sitting there with your heads in your hands, freaking out over the latest X-File that no one can (or wants to) solve? Just use Science. Johnny Science. Me.
